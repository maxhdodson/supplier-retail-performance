Weekly point-of-sale sales, margin, promotion, in-stock and shelf-space analysis for a consumer goods supplier.

Net sales by category

Last 52 weeks net sales, share of total, and change vs. the prior 52 weeks.

Regions

Sales per store indexed to the chain average (100 = average). Tick marks the chain average.

Weekly sales

Chain net sales per week, 4-week moving average, and the same week last year.

Promotion performance

Lift = promo-week units vs. the item's non-promo baseline (non-promo weeks within 4 weeks either side). Net incremental profit also pays for the discount on baseline units.

Out-of-stock impact

Estimated lost sales, last 52 weeks, top 8 items. Lost units = days out of stock x the item's units per in-stock day in the same region and week.

Shelf space

Sales per linear foot of shelf, indexed to the item's category (1.00 = category average). Add space at 1.25 or more, review space at 0.75 or less.
